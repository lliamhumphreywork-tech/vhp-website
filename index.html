01 · About

An independent investment bank, built around a gap nobody else was serving.

Most companies that are ready to list never do. The world's biggest banks won't touch them. The deals are too small. Local brokers can't reach the exchanges they need.

We built Von Hartmann & Partners specifically for that gap. We exist for one reason: to take growing companies public, onto the world's major exchanges, from anywhere in the world.

We get in early. We move fast. We see it through to listing day.

Headquartered in Sandton, Johannesburg, with active mandates and partnerships spanning the United States, the United Kingdom, Asia-Pacific and Africa. We are independent: no proprietary book, no syndicate desk, no competing positions.

Our edge is sequence: we find listing-ready companies before they engage a bank, and we compress months of manual assessment into days using a proprietary, AI-augmented diligence stack. Then we execute, human-led, mandate to bell.
